Picture: `2026-10-10-crossword-2-nyt-2015-10-12.webp`. The grid is 16 wide and
15 tall (the highlighted PROW is four squares of 27 pixels, and 438 pixels of
grid divided by 27 is sixteen, so I read it as sixteen columns, and the
180 degree symmetry of the black squares confirmed it). The two pictures sit
inside the grid: A (whiskey jar) at rows 5-7, columns 1-3; B (rocket labeled
Joe's Bakery) at rows 9-11, columns 14-16.

## The trick, solved
37-Across, row 8, sixteen letters: **A POCKET FULL OF RYE**.
Picture A is a jar of rye whiskey. 21-Across (row 4) is NURSERY RHYME,
"where Picture A might be found". Swap the two circled letters, P (column 2)
and R (column 14), and it reads **A ROCKET FULL OF PYE**: said aloud,
"a rocket full of pie", which is Picture B, the rocket from the bakery.
