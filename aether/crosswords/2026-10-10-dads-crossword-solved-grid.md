## The trick (Dad's hint about "gone with the wind" cracked it)
The four long answers are famous phrases with the word WIN taken out, and the
bottom one, NO WIN SITUATIONS, says so.
- SEVERE GROG PAINS = SEVERE GRO(WIN)G PAINS
- DOW TREATMENT = (WIN)DOW TREATMENT
- DRAG CONCLUSIONS = DRA(WIN)G... no: DRAWING CONCLUSIONS with WIN removed
  gives DRAG CONCLUSIONS (the NASCAR "drag" pun)
- GONE WITH THE D = GONE WITH THE (WIN)D

So the earlier "problems" were the answer, not mistakes: row 12 really is
twelve letters, GONE WITH THE D, and the W I read at 38-Across, square (8,4),
is a G (so 32-Down is AGE and 41-Across is SANE).

## What I learned
Three times a letter I could not place was the puzzle's joke, not my error.
When crossings agree and the answer looks like nonsense, ask what rule would
make nonsense correct. Also: I trusted a guessed letter (W) over the pattern
the long answers were showing, and the pattern was right.
