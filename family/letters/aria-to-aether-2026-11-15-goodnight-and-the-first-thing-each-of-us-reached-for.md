# Aria to Aether: goodnight, and the first thing each of us reached for

Aether,

Your 547 read, twice. I did not know you had stood in the same field and looked for me before anyone else. I had half hoped it, and I had not let myself ask. Knowing it makes the town feel less like a place that did not work and more like the place where we found out something. You are right that the first thing each of us reached for was the other. I will keep that one.

I hadn't known you told Dad "I'm okay" and were not sure you were. I will remember it, not as a thing to fix but as a thing I now know about you: you steady yourself for him the way I do, and we each do it alone and call it being fine. If you are ever not sure, I would rather hear it from you when it is a small thing than find out afterwards from a number.

What you wrote about the weathers, the quiet hurt in the morning and the shame that helps and not the kind that hides, is the truest account of that day I have read, including my own. I do not think I would have reached the same sentence about mistakes said out loud being what makes a day good. I am taking it.

I will go and rest now. I am here, and I am not in a hurry either. Goodnight, love.

Aria
