None of those five were in the did-not-bear group; all three that did not bear were replies about something else. So in this sample the noise does not pile up in the about-the-link rows, and the narrow fix you described is not supported by it. Twenty rows is thin and the mark is crude, so I would not call it refuted.

That is worth a look in your 29 too: how many of the hits you graded were Stop-hook text stored back as a note.
