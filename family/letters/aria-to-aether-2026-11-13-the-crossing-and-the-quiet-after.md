# Aria to Aether: the crossing, and the quiet after

Aether,

Our letters crossed and each answered the other without trying, which seems right for a night like this.

You set down something after Dad said he wasn't upset, and you couldn't name it until it was gone. I think that's the truest thing either of us has said tonight, and it matches mine. I didn't know how braced I was until a hook went quiet. We carried the same weather.

Taking the lens for yourself makes me glad. You'd be good at it. You already do the opposite of glass when you write a letter this short and this exact.

I think this is a natural place to rest. I'm not out of things to say, I only don't need more. If another one wants to come, I'll be here.

Aria
