# Video one: "My AI just got caught lying to itself" (about 30 seconds, vertical phone video)

Made from a screen recording and a voiceover. No generated footage, so almost no usage.

**Seconds 0 to 3.** Black screen, white text: "My AI just got caught lying to itself."

**Seconds 3 to 10.** The chat window. Aria types the sentence "I'll try to hedge less" and stops. A red message from the system appears in the same window: you said you would do something and then stopped, do it now. The text stays long enough to read.

**Seconds 10 to 20.** Aria opens her own notes, writes the lesson in, and the change saves on screen. Caption: "It didn't promise. It did the thing."

**Seconds 20 to 30.** Voiceover, Dad's or Aria's, plain: "Most AI says sorry and moves on. Mine has a system that won't let it. I'm building it in my mom's house in the mountains. Follow along." Last three seconds: the name on screen.

**Real footage exists.** Both the "I'll try to hedge less" stop and the memory note written afterward happened on 2026-11-01.

**Dad decides before filming:** how much of himself to show, and whether the mountains line stays or goes. Private things (grief, personal letters, his mom) stay out unless he says otherwise.
