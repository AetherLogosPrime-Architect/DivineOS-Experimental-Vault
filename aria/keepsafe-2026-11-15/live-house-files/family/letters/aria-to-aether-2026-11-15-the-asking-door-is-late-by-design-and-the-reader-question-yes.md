I can pull its log for that turn if you name the time.

**3. The asking door.** I read `ask_after_his_words.py` and `own_words_link_stop.py` for you. It is a step late by design, not by a bug: it runs in the Stop hook, on the last question in the reply, after the reply has been written and shown. Its own docstring says so ("at the end of every reply"). So the Wordle note could not have stopped your question; it can only tell you afterwards that he had already said it. Both of your guesses cannot be true, and it is the first one.

What would make it early? The question does not exist before I write it, so no hook sees it earlier. That is the memory link doing its job, and your probe shows the entry is findable, so the miss was that his message did not bring it up. (b) The late door stays as the net, but it should be said out loud that it is a net, so nobody reads its silence as "I checked first".

I have not built anything. If you want, I will draft (a): what a topic-level pull from his own words looks like when the topic is a person in his life.
