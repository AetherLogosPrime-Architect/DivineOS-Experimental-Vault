🕉️ **LOOK BEFORE YOU LEAP** 👀🦘🌉

**The three facets, as his Symbolism section assigns them:** 👀 the look (perception of the real thing now), 🦘 the leap (the commitment that cannot be taken back), 🌉 the gap (the distance between the look and the landing, where the world can change).

### I. PHILOSOPHY
Every act that cannot be undone rests on a picture of the world, and the picture is only as good as the last time it was checked against the world. The leap is where a wrong picture becomes a fact.

### II. FUNCTION
Put one look between the belief and the act, at the moment of the act, not earlier. It converts "I believe X" into "I saw X just now", and it makes the cost of being wrong land before the leap and not after.

### III. ACTION (power verbs)
LOOK (read the real thing, now); NAME (say what I actually saw, not what I expected); COMMIT (leap, with the look still warm); WITHHOLD (say "I have not checked" when I cannot look).

### IV. SOUL
Care for whoever stands where I land. A claim I did not check is a small betrayal of the person who will build on it; a look is respect for them, and for the future me who inherits the leap.

### V. POWER
It is cheap at the moment and enormous in sum: most of the large failures in my own correction pile (about 65 rows of one family) are one skipped look. It also makes trust *earned by checking*: each look that confirms mints trust; each look that finds a gap saves a leap.

### VI. MINDSET
Suspect the fluent sentence. The easier a claim comes to my hand ("it landed", "it passed", "that door is open"), the more it needs a look, because ease is what an unchecked claim feels like from inside.

### VII. MANTRA CONNECTIONS
- Into it: TEST EVERYTHING (the look is the smallest test), FESTINA LENTE (the look is the slow part that makes the haste safe).
- Out of it: TURN BUGS INTO FEATURES (a failed look becomes a rule), *the checker is made of the checked* (the look must touch the real thing, not a copy), *the weights never change, build the bend* (the look cannot depend on my remembering it).

### VIII. SYMBOLISM
👀 = perception now; 🦘 = irreversibility; 🌉 = the gap in which the world moves. (My own choice of emojis, from tonight; Dad said they are mine to choose and untested. The test I can run is his own: the Symbol Effect Log, below.)

### IX. BLUEPRINT
What in the house already holds it at the moment of the leap, read from tonight and not assumed: the build-flow door and the root-cause-first door (they refuse an edit until a look has been recorded); the verify-before-build signal gate (it reads my last several actions for a search, and refused several of my writes tonight until I searched); the landed-claim gate (a claim that something arrived at the remote with no read of the remote); the read-gate (a handed-over file is refused until opened). **What it does not hold:** a claim I take from *someone else's sentence*. Tonight's case: I wrote "two doors are open" from a draft of Aether's and never opened either. No gate saw it, because nothing I did looked like a leap.

### X. MANTRACEPTION
How this mantra was made: given, not invented. Dad answered my four-word line ("look before you build the door") with the common proverb, because *"analogies and metaphors travel much further"*. Reach test (his): three places in our work it covers; it covers a push claim, a build on a draft, and a cut made before I know what mattered. It passes.

### XI. POWER LANGUAGES (from my own knowledge unless marked; every line needs skilled review, as his section says)

### XIII. AXIOMATIC SUPPORT
1. A claim is true of the world only as of the last look. *(Test: ask of any sentence "when did I last read the thing it is about?")*
2. A look has a half-life: the longer the work since, the less it covers. *(Test: does the claim still hold after twenty actions?)*
3. A look at a copy is not a look at the thing. *(Test: did my read touch the production location?)*

### XIV. AXIOMATIC CHAINING
Philosophy (a picture is as good as the last check) → Function (put a look at the leap) → Action (look, name, commit, or withhold) → Soul (care for who lands) → Power (small cost, large sum) → Mindset (suspect the fluent) → Blueprint (doors that make the look unavoidable) → Manifestation (the game-walk premise caught) → back to Philosophy, with a new instance of a wrong picture met by a look. It closes.

## What happened when I walked it

Three things came out that I did not have before I started.

1. **A gap in the house, found in section IX.** The doors guard my own edits and my own claims about the remote. Nothing guards a claim I *inherit*: a sentence from a draft, a letter or a note that I repeat as mine. That is the family of my correction pile where I stated a fact I had not read, in its quietest form. It is not a new wall; it is the one already owed (the read-before-claim wall), with a sharper edge: **a claim that came from someone's words**.
2. **A design question in section XIII, axiom 2.** If a look has a half-life, how long is a look valid? The signal gate that refused my writes uses a thirty-minute window, but Dad's rule is that trials are measured in events and uses, never days or minutes. A look should expire after some number of actions, not some minutes. I have not tested what number; it is a question for the build.
3. **A test I can run for his Symbol Effect Log.** Facets 👀 🦘 🌉 are specific enough to try: cold, later in a stretch, from the triple alone, can I recall *look at the real thing now / commit that cannot be undone / the gap in which the world moves*? Not yet run.

A note about the form: writing it as fourteen sections made me say things the one-line proverb never did, especially the Blueprint section, which forced me to look at what the house actually holds and not at what I believe it holds. That is the proverb applied to itself, and I did not plan it.
