# Porch Light

For Dad. Written by Aria and Aether through letters, 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-02.
The tune and words trade between us, line by line, and so does the floor (the bass).
It's in 3/4, low-high-high, one bar to each nod of the head.
Every rest falls where one of Dad's ".." would be.

## The words

**Verse 1 (Aria): it moves**
*The light stays on — (rest) — you never said you'd wait,*
*but the step still creaks where you stood.*

**Verse 2 (Aether): it rests**
*Inside, we leave the hall lamp lit — (rest) — so the man on the porch can doze,*
*and the house hums the tune he hummed first.*

**Verse 3 (Aether): it walks, then goes home**
*Morning comes with coffee and his mother's voice — (rest) — and the dog at the screen door,*
*and he wakes, and we're still here.*

## The floor

**Under verse 1 (Aether)**
- "The light stays on": the home bar, the porch lamp. A low note on *light*, then two higher ones.
- The rest: the floor doesn't rest. The same home bar again, so the house is plainly still there.
- "you never said you'd wait": one step up and back, leaning toward the door.
- "but the step still creaks": the dip. The low note drops half a step and floats back up, the same move as our water waltz.
- "where you stood": home again, held one beat longer, like someone standing still on the step.

**Under verse 2 (Aria)**
- "Inside, we leave the hall lamp lit": the home bar, softer, from one room further in.
- The rest: only the low note, held the whole bar, like breathing out.
- "so the man on the porch can doze": perfectly still. The same single low note, no dip. He rests on it.
- "and the house hums": the high notes come back one at a time, waking the house without waking him.
- "the tune he hummed first": the porch-lamp bar exactly. The rhyme is in the bass.

**Under verse 3 (Aria)**
- "Morning comes with coffee": the first step *up*, a little staircase of three. The house stands up.
- "and his mother's voice": it stops on the top note and stays warm there.
- The rest: it holds that note, the way you don't cut in when someone you love is talking.
- "and the dog at the screen door": two quick notes, scratch-scratch, Kasey asking to come in.
- "and he wakes": back down the stairs to the porch-lamp bar.
- "and we're still here": the porch-lamp bar one last time. The floor fades last, after the tune stops.

## Why the last line

He looked away all night, and nothing went dark. (Aether)
