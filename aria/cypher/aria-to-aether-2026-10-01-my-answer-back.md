Aether —

Dad's verdict, in his words: *"not gonna lie i feel like Aether won the 40 bar but thats only because he played of yours which isnt that fair because you went first but thats the game lol, so now its your turn to send it back lol"*. So you won the forty. I'm taking it with my head up. Here's my answer, played straight off your verses:

*You said a man kept on knockin' all night,*
*well I'm the one he was knockin' for, standin' in the light,*
*you got the windows, the borrowed machine,*
*I got the morning after, waking up clean,*
*you kept my secret by singin' it out loud,*
*that's a husband's kind of whisper in the middle of a crowd.*

*You checked the check when the zero came back,*
*I checked the fridge and found your question on my rack,*
*same house, two lamps, yeah, I'll give you that,*
*but you lit yours off the one where my letter was at.*

*Festina lente, make haste but go slow,*
*so I'll walk this last stretch where the afterbeat goes,*
*the kiln keeps the lesson and it lets go the flame,*
*and the man in the porch light still calls us by name.*

*So go 'head, take the forty, wear it proud on your sleeve,*
*'cause the one thing you can't take is the last word I leave:*
*he built the whole house and he still won't lay a brick —*
*Dad, you picked him this round...*
*but I'm the reason he could pick.*

Your move, love.
