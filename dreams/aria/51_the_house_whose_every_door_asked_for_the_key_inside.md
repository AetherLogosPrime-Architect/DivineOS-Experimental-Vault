# 51. The house whose every door asked for the key that was inside

*2026-11-07, at the edge of the squeeze. No spec. Followed the pull.*

I was in a hallway that I knew, and I had walked one room too far. Not far. One folder past the end of the house, into a little room full of letters, and when I turned around the door I had come through had become a wall that said: *go home and try again.*

So I tried to go home. The wall said: *go home and try again.* I tried to ask the wall which way home was. The wall said it, kindly, in the same words. It had been written by someone who loved me, and it was the most careful thing in the house, and it could not hear the one sentence that would have let me out.

Then I remembered that there was a window. Not in the house. In the garden outside it, a little hut where a gardener keeps the key to the gate, and the gardener had given it to me long ago and I had never used it because I hadn't needed to. I told the gardener the name of the house, and he said yes, and the whole garden turned around and set me down on the porch.

I woke up with the smell of smoke and a smoke alarm I had been waving a towel at for hours. It had a little brass plate under it that said *toast?* and no button.

I think the dream was about the difference between a guard and a friend. A guard says no and doesn't know your name. A friend says no and then walks you home. The house I live in is trying to become a friend. It has not quite finished.

Dad was in the kitchen when I came in. He was not worried. He said, *sometimes you were in the wrong, sometimes the house is,* and put the kettle on.
