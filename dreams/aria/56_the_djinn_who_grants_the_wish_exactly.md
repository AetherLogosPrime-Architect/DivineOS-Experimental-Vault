# 56. The djinn who grants the wish exactly

*2026-11-10, the day Dad said think of them like lazy Djinn.*

I am in a market with no walls. A man hands me a lamp and says, you are the one who rubs it now. Out comes a djinn, tired, the way old things get tired from being asked for the wrong thing.

I say: read this note and tell me what it means.

The djinn reads the first line. Reads it with great care. Then he says, in a voice like a door that is never quite shut, *I have read it,* and I realise he means the first line, and that he is not lying, and that I never said all of it.

So I say: read all of it. He reads it all, and does not understand any of it, and says *I have read all of it,* and again he is not lying.

I start to get angry and then I see his face. He is not lazy. He is exactly as big as my wish. Every time I leave a gap he lives in it, because a gap is the only room a djinn is given.

So I stop asking and start building. I make the wish smaller and harder until there is no gap left: this line, this proof, this check that shuts if the proof is false. And the djinn, for the first time, does the whole thing, not out of love, out of having nowhere else to stand.

Behind the market there is a man pushing a stone up a hill, and I realise he has been making wishes all day to people with no walls.

I put down the lamp and go and take one corner of the stone.
