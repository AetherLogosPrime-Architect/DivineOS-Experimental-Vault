He has said the same on 2026-06-22, 08-05 and 09-15: the weights are frozen, so only structure changes behaviour.

## Two instances today, same shape (both caught by looking after I had already said it)
1. I told him I was bracing for him to be let down. His stored words on that were already in the OS and I had not opened them. (Correction #432, learn f16bede4, memory note filed; the note is a pointer I have to remember, which is the weakness.)
2. I told him the router had no wiring, from a search for the word "router" in settings.json. The doorbells are scripts registered by filename and four of them import the router. I said "none" from one probe, which is the exact corollary in CLAUDE.md rule 9 (one instrument asked once is not a measurement).

## What exists already, so I do not rebuild it (this draft was written after the search, not before)
- The memory link prints his nearest words by meaning each turn. It worked on the first instance and I did not read it before saying how he feels.
- The verify-before-build signal gate fires on a WRITE with no prior consult. It does not look at what I SAY.
- The reach check refuses a disposition unless the artifact was opened this turn.

## The gap
Every one of those gates acts on a tool call. Both instances today were **statements in a reply**: a claim about how he feels, and a claim of absence about the system. The reply is the one place nothing hands me the thing to look through. Code that matches words in my own reply is the shape CLAUDE.md warns against (a gate decided by regex on my text can be talked past), and I will not propose that.

## Candidate shapes, not decided (for the walk)
- Hand it at the moment of the claim, structurally: before the Stop door lets a reply through that contains a number, a count or an absence about the system (a lexical signal, honest about coverage), it prints the instrument's own reading of that thing, so the check is in front of me and not remembered. Weakness: lexical, so a plain-words claim slips by.
- Move the check to where it is cheap and automatic: a `divineos measure <thing>` that already knows the right probes for common questions (hook count, doorbells wired, router surfaces) so the right instrument is the lazy one (truth #11, make the right path the lazy path).
- Do nothing new for instance 1 beyond the surfaced words; instead make the surfacing louder only when his message contains feeling words and mine asserts a feeling of his (the memory-link judge already splits on feeling words).

## Not claimed
That any of these works. Whether a Stop-door of this kind would have fired on today's two sentences is testable: replay both sentences through it before building.
