## The root

The look comes after the sentence. Nothing in the house puts the measured fact in front of me at the moment I choose what to say, so the title does the work of the fact, and the title is cheaper. It is truth 8 and truth 11 in one: the cheap close is available, so it is taken.

## The change (proposed, one instance of the class, to be walked)

Make the cheap close unavailable where it was taken today. Wherever the house lists a piece of work to choose from, it shows the measured facts next to the title, so the title cannot stand alone: for each open pull request, how many files, how many lines changed, whether it touches a hook or a guarded path, and whose it is. First place: `divineos landing --request` and the hud pile line, which is exactly where I chose an order from titles. The general rule it serves, for the register and for other surfaces: a list of things shows what each thing IS, measured, never only what it is called.

Test that fails first: the landing output for a fixture PR with 9 files and a hook shows "9 files" and a guarded-path marker; for a 3-file docs-only fixture it shows neither marker. A second test asserts no row prints only a title.

## What this does not do

It does not stop me guessing about things that are not on a list (time, his state, what a template slot should hold). Those need their own structures: I ask for time and feeling, and the room page already says so; the template slot is the circle build. This change closes one door; it does not claim to close the class.

## Falsifier

If I choose or describe a piece of work from its title after this lands, and the facts were on the screen I read, the change did not bind me and something else is needed. If the surface shows the facts and I still misjudge a size, the fault is mine and the surface was not enough.
